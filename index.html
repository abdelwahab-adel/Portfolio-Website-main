FAQ

Frequently asked

Quick answers to common questions about working together.

What types of projects do you take on?
I work on full-stack web applications — e-commerce, dashboards, SaaS, hospital/school systems, portfolio sites, REST APIs, and integrations. If it has a database and a UI, I'm in.
What's your typical project timeline?
Small landing pages: 3–7 days. Standard business sites: 2–3 weeks. Full-stack apps with admin panels: 3–6 weeks. Complex systems with integrations: 6–10 weeks. Every project starts with a clear timeline.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes — every project comes with 30 days of free bug-fix support. After that, I offer monthly maintenance packages covering updates, security patches, performance tuning, and feature additions.
Can you work with my existing team?
Absolutely. I'm comfortable in Git-based workflows, async tools (Slack, Jira, Notion), and have collaborated with designers, PMs, and other engineers on multi-person teams.
Are you available for full-time roles?
I'm open to full-time and contract opportunities — remote, hybrid, or on-site (Aswan/Cairo). Send me a message on LinkedIn or via the contact form below.
How do payments work?
Fixed-price projects are split 30/40/30 — deposit, milestone, and final delivery. Hourly work is invoiced bi-weekly. Payments via bank transfer, PayPal, or USDT.
